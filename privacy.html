Privacy policy

"rclone (personal)" is an OAuth client used by a single person, its owner, to mount their own Google Drive on their own computer with rclone.

  • What it accesses: the files and file metadata in the signed-in user's Google Drive, through the Google Drive API.
  • Where the data goes: only between Google Drive and the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any other server, and the developer runs no server of their own.
  • What is stored: the sign-in token, kept in an encrypted rclone configuration file on the owner's computer, and copies of recently used files in a local cache.
  • Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone, and none is used for advertising or analytics.
  • Revoking access: remove "rclone" at myaccount.google.com/connections.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
